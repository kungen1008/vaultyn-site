If one day you can’t, how would your family open your accounts? That’s what the emergency sheet is for.

## Print it

Go to **Settings → Import & export → Print emergency sheet**. You can choose:

- **Which categories** to print, or only favorites;
- **What to include**: passwords, linked emails and phones, security questions, unused backup codes, two-factor secrets or QR codes, notes;
- Whether to leave **blank lines** to hand-write the master password and recovery key.

The printed sheet also explains how to restore the data.

## Keep in mind

The printed sheet is a **plain-text password book**:

- Print it on your own home printer — **don’t “Save as PDF”** to your computer or cloud drive.
- Hand-write the master password and recovery key in the blank lines; don’t print them.
- Keep it somewhere safe that your family knows about, such as a safe.
- It only reflects what was in the vault when printed. Reprint after changing important passwords.
