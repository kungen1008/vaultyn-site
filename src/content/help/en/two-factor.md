## Add a two-factor secret

When you turn on two-factor authentication, the website usually shows a QR code, often with a link like “Can’t scan it?” that reveals a **secret** (letters and digits).

Edit the account, paste the secret (or an `otpauth://` link) into **Two-factor secret** and save. Vaultyn then shows the same 6-digit codes as your phone’s authenticator.

## Use it on your phone too

In the account details, click **QR code** and scan it with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar to get the same codes on your phone.

## Backup codes

Sites usually give you a set of single-use backup (recovery) codes. Paste them into the account’s **Backup codes**, tick each one off as you use it, and always see how many are left.

## Security questions

Some sites ask for security questions. Add **Security questions** to the account; the answers are encrypted just like passwords.
