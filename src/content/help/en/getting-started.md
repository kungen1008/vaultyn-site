Here’s how to get going the first time you open Vaultyn.

## 1. Set a master password

The master password is the only key to your vault — at least 8 characters. A sentence only you know, such as a few words strung together, works well: **length matters more than complexity**.

You can add a password hint to jog your memory, but the hint is stored unencrypted, so **never put the password itself in it**.

## 2. Save the recovery key

After you create the vault, Vaultyn shows a **recovery key**. It’s the only way to reset a forgotten master password.

- It’s **shown only once** — write it down or print it, and keep it somewhere safe.
- Keep it with your [emergency sheet](../emergency-sheet/), for example in a home safe.

## 3. Confirm the data folder

Vaultyn saves encrypted data in its private folder on this Mac by default. For multi-Mac sync, go to **Settings → Data & sync** and change it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. See [Data folder & iCloud Drive sync](../data-folder/).

## 4. Add accounts

Click **＋** at the top right, choose a type (login, card, ID, Wi-Fi…), fill it in and save. Or bring everything over at once: see [Import from other apps](../import/).

## Everyday actions

- **Search**: ⌘K, or just press /
- **Copy password**: select an entry and press ⌘C; **copy account**: ⌘B
- **Lock**: click the lock at the top right, or press the global shortcut ⌘⌥L
