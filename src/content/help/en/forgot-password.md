## Reset with the recovery key

1. On the lock screen, click **Forgot master password?**
2. Enter the **recovery key** you saved when you created the vault.
3. Set a new master password.

## If the recovery key is lost too

Unfortunately the data **can’t be opened**, and we can’t help either. Vaultyn has no server and no back door — that’s exactly what makes it yours alone.

To avoid this in future:

- Once Vaultyn is open, you can change the master password in **Settings → Security**.
- Print the recovery key together with your [emergency sheet](../emergency-sheet/) and keep them somewhere safe.
